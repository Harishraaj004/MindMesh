# Vendor Review: Turbidity Probes

Halden Sensor costs 1,850 dollars per probe and carries a two year warranty. The optical window is not self-cleaning, which explains the algae fouling seen in Project Aurora pilots.

Brightwater Probe costs 2,400 dollars per probe and includes a wiper that reduces fouling drift to under 1 percent per month. Brightwater Probe has a six month lead time.

Recommendation from procurement: keep Halden Sensor for the current budget cycle and revisit Brightwater Probe if Drift Correction cannot hold error under 3 percent.
