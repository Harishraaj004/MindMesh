# Project Aurora: Research Notes

Project Aurora is a river water-quality monitoring network led by Priya Nair. The network uses Halden Sensor probes to measure turbidity, dissolved oxygen and pH at four sites: Riverside Station, Millbrook Weir, North Bridge and Cedar Outfall.

Early pilots showed that Halden Sensor turbidity readings drift upward by roughly 4 percent per month when the optical window fouls with algae. Drift Correction is therefore applied to every reading before it enters the analysis dataset.

Riverside Station records the highest turbidity because of nearby construction runoff. Priya Nair believes the construction site at Riverside Station explains the spike seen in March, but this is not yet confirmed by upstream sampling.

The team compared two calibration strategies. Weekly Two-Point Calibration keeps error under 2 percent but costs two technician hours per site. Automated Drift Correction relies on a reference cell and requires no site visits.
